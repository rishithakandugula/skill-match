❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

Here are some quick answers to common questions.

How does SkillMatch recommend courses?

SkillMatch analyzes your current skills, proficiency levels and career goals. It then identifies skill gaps and recommends courses that can help you improve.

How is my skill-match percentage calculated?

The match percentage compares your current skills with the skills required for a specific course, internship or job opportunity.

Can I update my skills later?

Yes. You can add, edit or remove skills from your Skills page and update your proficiency levels as you continue learning.

Can SkillMatch help me find internships?

Yes. The Internships section displays opportunities according to your skills, experience and career interests.

How can I improve my career readiness?

Complete recommended courses, build projects, improve weak skills and complete assessments. Your career-readiness score can then improve.

Is SkillMatch suitable for beginners?

Yes. SkillMatch is designed for students and beginners as well as experienced learners. You can start with beginner-level skills and gradually progress toward advanced roles.
